## I'll think of a title... (I won't)

You are witnessing the start of the next frontier in human thought. Bare witness to how I can't even decide which song to pick. I chose Metallica's - One because today on TikTok I saw an exquisite Anakin edit using the song. I've never really had much interest in video editing but watching that definitely made me curious at trying my hand. The HSM song I feel is pretty self explanatory. It's the very first day and I'm already breaking rules... what kind of precedent is that setting for myself. In fact, just look at that last sentence and I'm still relying on my iconic crutch of the ellipsis. I feel like that gives me a good opportunity to speak about some of the reasons I decided to start this site as it ties into my ABUNDANT use of ellipses.

1. **I'm an illiterate fuck.** I think I've always struggled to write in a way that doesn't make me sound like I'm recovering from a concussion. So I plan to write daily to this site with the hope that it leads to at least SOMETHING beneficial. I have never used markdown extensively which is what I'm using for this site. So I hope to gain some markdown literacy and familiarity as well in the process.
2. **I'm a sentimental fuck.** Recently, I visited my nan's house which is chock full of photographs throughout her life. Every night, I see on my bedstand my class photo from when I was 7. I wouldn't say it's a rare thing for me to feel emotional, most things send me to tears, but there is something about keeping in touch with the past that really strikes a chord with me. If I do decide to stick with this you'll probably even see this ingrained within things I'm passionate for, like media preservation. The main benefit I see this site holding for myself is that I'll be able to look back in the future and read some of my thoughts and feelings.

These are probably things that should be said on the home page or an about page instead of here but this is the starting line and I'm still trying to find my footing. THe layout of things is heavily subject to change at this point in time. I'll probably just keep this first page brief and end it here for today.
